# Changelog

## [1.2.0 Preview 1](https://github.com/Niklex21/still/releases/tag/v1.2.0-preview.1)

Released November 16, 2026 (build 31).

- Move the screenshot shelf by dragging the center grip. The bar expands as you approach, and the cursor changes from an open hand to a closed hand as you drag.
- Click Collapse or press **⌥⇧−** to tuck the shelf out of the way. Press the shortcut again to restore your queue. You can change it in Settings → Shortcuts.
- Open History from the clock on the shelf. History now loads thumbnails as you browse, making it faster to open and close large libraries.
- Drag screenshots into upload fields, including the Lovable composer, as temporary PNG files. A successful drop removes the preview and keeps the original in History.
- Area capture shows a crosshair while you aim and drag. Starting another area capture replaces an unfinished selection.

## [1.1.0 Preview 2](https://github.com/Niklex21/still/releases/tag/v1.1.0-preview.2)

Released November 9, 2026.

- Quick Start opens on first launch and shows your configured shortcuts. Help lets you reopen the guide or prepare a bug report.
- Hold Option over a thumbnail for alternate actions, including Prepare for Upload, Copy Text, full-size copy, and immediate Trash.
- Added signed in-app updates for future releases and optional usage counts. Usage reporting defaults to off.

## [1.1.0 Preview 1](https://github.com/Niklex21/still/releases/tag/v1.1.0-preview.1)

Released November 8, 2026.

- Scrolling captures show a live preview beside the selected area. Stitching handles sticky headers and floating buttons.
- Area capture freezes the screen when selection starts. Added private and repeat-region captures.
- Using the last pending screenshot hides the automatic shelf. Added sharing presets, a hover shortcut for copying for sharing, and optional history retention.
- Added editor zoom and pan, inline text editing, aligned image comparisons, and support for longer walkthrough captions.

## [1.0.0 Preview 1](https://github.com/Niklex21/still/releases/tag/v1.0.0-preview.1)

Released November 6, 2026.

- First public universal DMG. Capture a region, window, screen, or scrolling area and drag screenshots from a floating queue.
- Annotate with editable shapes and numbered pins, copy text with OCR, compare images, or combine a batch into a walkthrough.
- Search local history by text, metadata, or visual descriptions. Includes customizable shortcuts and optional Raycast integration.
